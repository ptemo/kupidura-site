KUPIDURA

Kupidura is a composer and pianist, arranger, lyricist and vocalist. He is the creator of Scènes inachevées (Unfinished Scenes), a cycle of piano works. He also composes ambient music, occasionally sings, and enjoys collaborating with other musicians by writing songs for them.

Available for concerts, events and special occasions in Warsaw, across Poland and throughout Europe.
