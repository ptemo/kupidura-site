KUPIDURA
Composer & pianist
Warsaw, Poland

Kupidura is a Warsaw-based minimalist composer and pianist, also working as an arranger, lyricist and vocalist. He is the creator of Scènes inachevées (Unfinished Scenes), a cycle of piano works. His music is built around intimacy, repetition and atmosphere, moving between contemporary classical and cinematic sound. He also composes ambient music and collaborates with other musicians by writing songs for them.
