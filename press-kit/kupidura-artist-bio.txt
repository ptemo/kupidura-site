KUPIDURA
Composer & pianist
Warsaw, Poland

Kupidura is a Warsaw-based composer and pianist whose intimate piano music blends contemporary classical minimalism with a cinematic sense of atmosphere. He is the creator of Scènes inachevées (Unfinished Scenes), an ongoing cycle of piano works shaped by repetition and evolving moods. He is also a professor at Warsaw University of Technology, where his academic work focuses on artificial intelligence and satellite imagery. Alongside his solo piano work, Kupidura composes ambient music and collaborates with other artists as an arranger, lyricist and vocalist.
