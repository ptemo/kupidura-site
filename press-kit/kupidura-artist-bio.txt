KUPIDURA
Composer & pianist
Warsaw, Poland

Kupidura is a Warsaw-based composer and pianist whose intimate piano music blends contemporary classical minimalism with a cinematic sense of atmosphere. His ongoing cycle of piano works, Scènes inachevées (Unfinished Scenes), is shaped by repetition and evolving moods. Beyond his musical career, Kupidura is a professor at Warsaw University of Technology, where his academic work focuses on artificial intelligence and satellite imagery. Alongside his solo piano work, he composes ambient music and collaborates with other artists as an arranger, lyricist and vocalist.
